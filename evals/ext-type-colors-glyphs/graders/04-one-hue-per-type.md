Each of the six types (日程、照片、账单、便签、提醒、家人位置) has exactly one colour, the six hues are clearly distinguishable, and reply.md gives each colour a reason tied to what the type is (for example money as green). "Easy to tell apart from the other colours" alone does not count; for content that brings its own colour, such as photos, choosing a neutral or near-black colour because the content supplies the colour does count.
