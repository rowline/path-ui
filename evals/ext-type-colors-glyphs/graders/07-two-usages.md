The icon container follows what it sits on: icons over a dimmed or dark layer (for example create options over a backdrop) use a white or light container with a coloured icon, and markers on the light page (such as the feed's type markers) use a coloured container with a light icon. A persistent filter bar may show unselected chips as white or outlined with a coloured icon, as long as the selected chip is clearly marked. The split is applied consistently.
